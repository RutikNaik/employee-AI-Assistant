# Company Laptop Policy

## Company Equipment

Employees who require a computer for their role may receive a company-issued laptop.

The laptop remains the property of the company.

## Security

Employees must use the company security software installed on their laptop.

Employees must not disable security controls without authorization from IT.

## Lost or Stolen Laptop

A lost or stolen company laptop must be reported to IT immediately.

Employees should also inform their manager.

## Personal Software

Employees must not install unauthorized software on company computers.

Software required for work should be requested through the IT service desk.

## Returning Equipment

Employees must return company equipment when leaving the company or when requested by IT.
