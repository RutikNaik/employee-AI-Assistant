# Acme Analytics – Employee HR Policy (SAMPLE)


> Fictional sample document for testing. Not a real company policy.


Version: 3.2 | Effective: 1 April 2026 | Owner: People Operations (hr@acme-analytics.example)


## 1. Working Hours

- Standard week: 40 hours, Monday to Friday.

- Core hours: 11:00–16:00 local time. Employees must be available during core hours.

- Overtime must be pre-approved by the line manager and is compensated as time off in lieu (TOIL) at 1:1.


## 2. Leave


| Leave type | Entitlement per year | Notes |

|---|---|---|

| Annual leave | 24 working days | Accrues 2 days per month. Max 5 days carry forward to next year, must be used by 31 March. |

| Sick leave | 12 days | Medical certificate required for more than 2 consecutive days. |

| Casual leave | 6 days | Max 2 consecutive days. Cannot be combined with annual leave. |

| Maternity leave | 26 weeks paid | Available after 80 days of service. |

| Paternity leave | 10 working days paid | Must be taken within 3 months of birth or adoption. |

| Bereavement leave | 5 working days | Immediate family. |

| Public holidays | 10 days | Published by HR each December for the following year. |


### 2.1 Applying for leave

- Apply in the HR portal at least **5 working days** in advance for annual leave over 2 days.

- Managers must approve or reject within 2 working days.

- Unapproved absence is treated as unpaid leave.


## 3. Remote and Hybrid Work

- Hybrid employees work from the office at least **2 days per week** (team decides which days).

- Fully remote roles must be approved by the department head and HR.

- Working from another country is allowed for up to **20 working days per year**, with written approval from HR and Legal at least 15 days before travel.

- Home office allowance: one-time USD 300 for equipment, plus USD 40 per month for internet.


## 4. Expenses and Travel

- Submit expense claims within **30 days** of spending, with receipts.

- Meal limit on business travel: USD 60 per day.

- Economy class for flights under 6 hours; premium economy allowed for flights over 6 hours.

- Claims over USD 1,000 need approval from the department head.


## 5. Probation and Notice Period


| Stage | Duration / Notice |

|---|---|

| Probation | 6 months (can be extended once by 3 months) |

| Notice during probation | 2 weeks |

| Notice after confirmation (individual contributors) | 1 month |

| Notice after confirmation (managers and above) | 2 months |


Unused annual leave may be adjusted against the notice period only with manager approval.


## 6. Code of Conduct

- Treat colleagues, clients and partners with respect. Harassment and discrimination of any kind are not tolerated.

- Declare conflicts of interest to your manager and HR within 7 days of becoming aware of them.

- Gifts from clients or vendors above USD 50 must be declared.


## 7. Grievances and Escalation

1. Raise the issue with your line manager.

2. If unresolved in 10 working days, raise it with your HR Business Partner.

3. If still unresolved, escalate to the Head of People (people-head@acme-analytics.example).

4. Anonymous reports: Ethics hotline, available 24/7.

## 8. Learning and Development

- Each employee has a learning budget of **USD 1,000 per year**.

- Certifications over USD 500 need manager approval. If the employee leaves within 12 months, the cost is recovered pro rata.

- Payroll: payroll@acme-analytics.example
