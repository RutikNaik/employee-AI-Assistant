# New Employee Onboarding Guide

## Before Joining

HR will provide the employee with joining instructions and required documentation.

IT will prepare required accounts and equipment.

## First Day

On the first day, employees should:

1. Complete HR documentation.
2. Collect their company laptop if applicable.
3. Activate their company email.
4. Complete required security training.
5. Meet their manager.
6. Review their team responsibilities.

## First Week

New employees should complete mandatory training during their first week.

Their manager should explain team processes, communication channels, and current priorities.

## IT Support

Employees who experience technical problems should contact the IT service desk.

## HR Support

Questions about benefits, leave, payroll, and employee policies should be directed to HR.
