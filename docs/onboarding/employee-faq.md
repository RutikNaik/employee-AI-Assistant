# Employee Frequently Asked Questions

## How do I request leave?

Submit a leave request through the HR system and wait for manager approval.

## Who should I contact about salary questions?

Contact the HR or Payroll team.

## Who should I contact if my laptop stops working?

Contact the IT service desk.

## Can I work from another country?

Employees may work from another country for up to 20 working days per year, subject to HR and Legal approval.

## How do I report a security incident?

Contact the IT Security team immediately.

## Where can I find company policies?

Company policies are stored in the approved internal document repository.

## Who handles workplace grievances?

Employees should initially raise grievances with their HR Business Partner.

If the issue cannot be resolved, it may be escalated to the Head of People.
