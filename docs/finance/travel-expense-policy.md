# Business Travel and Expense Policy

## Meal Expenses

Employees travelling for approved business purposes may claim meal expenses up to USD 60 per day.

Expenses above the daily limit require additional approval.

## Hotel

Hotel accommodation should normally be booked through the company's approved travel system.

## Transportation

Reasonable transportation expenses related to approved business travel may be reimbursed.

## Receipts

Employees must provide receipts for reimbursable expenses when required by the expense system.

## Expense Submission

Travel expenses should normally be submitted within 15 calendar days after returning from the trip.

## Approval

Expenses must be reviewed and approved by the employee's manager.
