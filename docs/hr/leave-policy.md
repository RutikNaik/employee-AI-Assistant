# Employee Leave Policy

## Annual Leave

Employees receive 24 days of annual leave per calendar year.

Up to 5 unused annual leave days can be carried forward to the following year.

Carried-forward leave must be used by 31 March. Any remaining carried-forward leave after 31 March will expire.

## Sick Leave

Employees receive 12 sick leave days per year.

A medical certificate may be required when an employee takes more than 3 consecutive working days of sick leave.

## Emergency Leave

Employees may request up to 3 emergency leave days per year.

Emergency leave should be reported to the manager as soon as reasonably possible.

## Parental Leave

Eligible employees may receive parental leave according to applicable company policy and local law.

## Leave Approval

Employees should submit planned leave requests through the HR system.

Managers are responsible for approving or rejecting leave requests.

Employees should not assume leave is approved until they receive confirmation.
