# Attendance and Working Hours Policy

## Working Hours

The standard working schedule is Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

Employees receive a one-hour lunch break.

## Late Arrival

Employees who expect to arrive more than 30 minutes late should inform their manager.

Repeated unexplained late arrivals may be discussed with the employee and HR.

## Remote Attendance

Employees working remotely must remain available during their agreed working hours.

Employees should attend required meetings and respond to work communications in a reasonable timeframe.

## Absence

Unexpected absence should be reported to the employee's manager as soon as possible.

Unreported absence may be referred to HR for review.
