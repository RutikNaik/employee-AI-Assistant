# Work From Home Policy

## Eligibility

Employees may work remotely when their role is suitable for remote work and their manager has approved the arrangement.

## Working From Another Country

Employees may work from another country for up to 20 working days per calendar year.

Working from another country requires approval from both HR and Legal before the employee begins working there.

## Home Office

Employees working remotely are expected to maintain a professional and secure workspace.

Company confidential information must not be accessible to unauthorized people.

## Meetings

Employees working remotely are expected to attend scheduled meetings unless they have an approved reason for absence.

## Equipment

Company-issued laptops and other equipment remain company property and must be protected from theft, damage, and unauthorized access.
